Tutoring

Online, via Zoom

I tutor mostly maths, physics, and logic, and have also tutored general philosophy of physics and philosophy of science. I've taught students from primary school age, through sixth-form to undergraduate and graduate level, though in recent years this has mostly been undergraduates. All sessions are held over Zoom.

Over the past several years, this has included tutoring students taking the following modules at the following universities, among others:

Imperial College London
Solid State Physics, Astrophysics, Mathematical Methods
